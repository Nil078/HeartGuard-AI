Model Selection Criteria

Selected Logistic Regression: combined test-set score emphasising recall (weight 0.35) together with ROC-AUC (0.30), F1 (0.20), and accuracy (0.15). In screening contexts, prioritising sensitivity (90.91%) minimises false negative errors while maintaining superior discriminative capability (0.9026 AUC).

Academic Capstone

About HeartGuard AI

B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering capstone project exploring ML for preventative clinical decision support.

Problem Statement & Objectives

Cardiovascular diseases (CVDs) represent the leading cause of global mortality. Early, accessible, and objective risk stratification enables timely lifestyle interventions before irreversible cardiac events occur.

HeartGuard AI translates complex patient physiological profiles into transparent risk indications using audited machine learning pipelines, feature attribution metrics, and rule-based clinical guidance.

Dataset Specification

  • Source: UCI Cleveland Heart Disease Cohort (303 patient samples)
  • Target: Binary angiographic detection (>50% vessel narrowing)
  • Protocol: Stratified 80/20 split with pipeline-isolated median/mode imputation
